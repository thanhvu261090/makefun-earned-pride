## The entry

> She sounded out the last word of her grandson's letter, folded it into her apron pocket, and walked on to the market like nothing at all had happened.

A quiet, earned moment of pride: a grandmother who has taught herself to read finishes her grandson's letter — a long effort finally landing — and carries on with her day as if it were nothing.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) holds the entry as a constant, checks it against the competition gate — UTF-8 text of 1 to 240 characters, not only white space, a single line — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
